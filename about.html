The Palm organizer you loved, now on your phone.

Agendus was the agenda app on Palm OS: your appointments, tasks, contacts and notes on one clever "today" screen. Agendus Web brings it back as a modern web app. Free, open source, works offline, and your data stays on your device.

What's inside

Agenda screen

Today's appointments, tasks, birthdays and the people you're meeting, on one page.

Every calendar view

Day, week, month, year and list. Drag appointments to move them, pull the edge to resize.

To Do, contacts, memos

Priorities, repeating tasks, contact history and quick notes, all linked together.

Three looks

Glossy 2007 (light and night), clean Modern, and Classic Palm with the green-grey screen.

Private by default

No account, no tracking. Everything is stored in your browser and works offline.

Optional extras

Sync between your devices via your own small server, and a voice assistant with your own AI key.

Get involved

This is a hobby project and it would love company. Developers, designers, translators and Palm veterans are all welcome.

Ideas on the wish list

  • More languages (it speaks English and Dutch today)
  • Accessibility improvements
  • Calendar subscriptions and CalDAV sync
  • End-to-end encrypted sync
  • A wide "Palm Desktop" layout for tablets and computers
  • Handwriting input in the spirit of Graffiti

How to join

Try the demo and open an issue with what you found or what you'd like to build. Read the contributing guide for how the code is set up: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no build step.

Still have the original Agendus on an old Palm, or screenshots and manuals? Those help a lot too.
